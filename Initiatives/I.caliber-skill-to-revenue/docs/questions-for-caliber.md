## Call plan

Let him walk. Watch first, questions second. If time is ~30 minutes: 15 product, 10 problem, 5 logistics.

### 1. Walkthrough — jobs to be done (watch, then ask)

You are hiring this dashboard to do a job. The stills do not show the job. Ask these while looking at the screen, not as a list.

- When does a front-line manager open this? Monday with the director, a bad forecast, a 1:1, never?
- What does “done” look like when they leave? A name, a skill, an action, a Slack to the rep?
- Who asked them to look — themselves, or someone above them?
- After Skill Report: the Priority action slot is empty. What is supposed to happen there today? What do managers actually do instead?
- Evaluations already have a CRM deal name. Does anyone use that column? Do they bounce to Salesforce after, or is this a dead end?
- If Tara is the scene: can this product help him at all today, or does he still have to listen to every call?

**Watch for:** does he narrate *skill gaps* or *deals and money*? That tells you which job they think they are shipping. If he never mentions a deal until you ask, the current product is still a scoreboard, and connecting skill to deals is net-new in usage, not just in UI.

### 2. Problem — why this, why now (the ones that change the bet)

- If Ellis could tell fit from skill on named deals and show a six-week score trend, with no dollar on the page, would that still be a win for this exercise — or does the primary screen have to speak revenue because that is how managers get airtime with their director / how Caliber renews?
- Who is the first person this “skill → deals → revenue” story has to satisfy: Ellis, Ellis’s director, Reid (enablement budget), Jordan (board), or Caliber CS on a renewal call?
- The brief lists a lot of downstream uses (CRO, hiring, ramp, deal-risk). For a manager-primary surface, which of those is a roll-up of the same objects, and which would you rather I not pretend to solve?
- Current State says you are aware of amount / win-loss / stage progression but do not use them, and that we may correlate. Fair to put those on a manager deal list in this sample?

### 3. Mechanics — only if they change a screen
