## Summary

- **Title:** Principal Product Designer
- **Base salary range:** $175,000–$225,000. Listed as salary range. The posting does not say whether that is base or total.
- **Location:** Lehi office, on-site. Los Angeles and San Francisco are the other offices. Remote work days are a perk. The posting does not say how many days are in Lehi.
- **Company:** Outsmart is an early-stage startup building a new college experience for the AI era, aimed at students who want access without the cost and shape of a traditional university. Former Duolingo executives lead it. The posting says the company is backed by $38 million, including Khosla Ventures, Forerunner, DST Global Partners, and Lightspeed.
- **What stands out:** This is a hands-on design leadership seat. Most of the time is designing and shipping, and the role also mentors designers, runs critique, and evolves the design system and product language. Consumer education, social, or gaming experience is a bonus. They cite 1–5 million weekly organic views.
- **Years of experience:** 8+ years of product design, with significant consumer product work, plus experience managing and developing product designers
- **Recently noteworthy:** On October 23, 2025, Outsmart posted that it had closed a $25 million seed round led by DST Global and Forerunner, with Khosla Ventures and Lightspeed participating (company LinkedIn).
- **Case study to focus on:** FamilySearch Personalized Discovery, https://codybduke.github.io/CodyBDukePortfolio/work/familysearch-discovery. It is consumer work at scale, a reason to come back, and a shared system other designers followed. For the leadership question, tell the Product Lead year. That story is on the resume and is not its own public case. If they want the design-system case, open Entrata Pricing Specials, https://codybduke.github.io/CodyBDukePortfolio/work/pricing-specials.
